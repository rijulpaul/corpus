# Narrative Game Mechanics
## Core Concept
- Narrative Game Mechanics (NGM):
    - Game mechanics that invite player and AI actions supporting the construction of engaging mental stories in the player’s mind.
    - Narrative emerges through interaction, not just scripted storytelling.

- Cognitive Narratology Basis:
    - Stories are mental constructs, formed in real time as players act and react in a game world.
    - Thus, games can generate narratives dynamically through gameplay mechanics rather than through retelling.

## Theoretical Foundation
- Moves away from classical narratology (story as past events told by a narrator).
- Embraces a cognitive definition: narrative arises as the player’s mind connects events meaningfully during play.
- Mechanics and rules shape the player’s behavior, guiding but not determining the story that unfolds.
- Mechanics = verbs (actions possible in a system). Rules define conditions and results.

## Purpose of Narrative Game Mechanics
NGMs:
- Create story events (not fixed storylines).
- Work with other narrative devices (environmental storytelling, dialogue, audio, etc.).
- Allow embodied narrative construction — players feel and think through play.

## Case Studies and Findings
### Left 4 Dead 2
- Theme: Survival through teamwork.
- Mechanics as story drivers: Moving, attacking, healing, incapacitation.
- Narrative Effect: Mechanics create tension and spatial conflict—e.g., separation from teammates increases suspense.
- Insight: Well-designed mechanics and AI behaviors can naturally create recurring “story events” (loss, rescue, teamwork).

### The Last of Us: Left Behind
- Theme: Friendship and emotional survival.
- Mechanics reused contextually: Same mechanics (throwing, shooting) used both for combat and character-building (brick-throwing contest, water-gun fight).
- Narrative Effect: Evokes empathy and reveals character depth through gameplay, not cutscenes.
- Insight: Changing context, not mechanics, can shift gameplay from action to emotional storytelling.

### Papers, Please
- Theme: Morality, bureaucracy, and survival.
- Mechanics: Inspect, approve/deny, manage time and income.
- Narrative Effect: Mechanics generate moral dilemmas (law vs. empathy, duty vs. family).
- Insight: Novel mechanics can create original narrative experiences beyond action genres.

## Conclusions
- Mechanics and rules influence the player’s behavior, shaping the emergent story world.
- Three main narrative functions of mechanics:
  - Build tension through spatial conflict.
  - Evoke empathy through characterization.
  - Create moral dilemmas through choice.
- Future direction: Study how player interaction patterns (repetition, mastery, meaningful choice) affect character behavior and narrative coherence.

## Core Takeaway
Narrative Game Mechanics are not about telling a story but about designing systems that make players act out stories—where meaning, emotion, and fiction emerge from gameplay itself.
